# SourceLoop v0.3.1 — Governed Active-Market Investigation

This release expands SourceLoop from direct-source correspondence and quote acquisition into a pack-driven investigation platform for current prices, operational practices, public business records, license representations, market coverage, and disclosure completeness.

## New capabilities

- `market_investigation` case kind with quote-probe, practice-audit, compliance-probe, market-census, and record-verification modes.
- Explicit low, standard, elevated, and restricted risk tiers.
- Required governance acknowledgements, institutional-only gates, public/business-contact restrictions, and sensitive-data prohibitions.
- Structured investigation findings with severity, status, source scope, evidence lineage, review requirements, and reviewer receipts.
- Registry-check records that distinguish verified, matched, not-found, conflicting, and unavailable outcomes without automatically accusing a subject of illegality or misconduct.
- Pack-driven response coverage and narrowly scoped, approval-gated follow-ups for unanswered critical fields.
- Deterministic lender arithmetic checks labeled as comparison estimates rather than legal APR calculations.
- Deterministic staffing bill-rate/pay-rate markup analysis and mismatch review.
- JSON and CSV case-report exports.
- Investigation findings, registry checks, review state, and risk metadata in the relationship/GIS graph.
- An expanded operator console for packs, investigation mode, risk tier, findings, reviews, reports, and correspondence state.

## Deliberate boundaries

The platform does not create fake applicants, borrowers, residents, customers, or constituents; submit applications; provide sensitive personal data; accept credit or commercial offers; transfer funds; sign contracts; expose residential locations; or treat a missing registry match as proof of unlawful conduct.

External communication remains governed by truthful requester identity, automation disclosure, approved contact routes, suppression controls, per-pack contact and follow-up ceilings, deterministic policy evaluation, and operator approval.
